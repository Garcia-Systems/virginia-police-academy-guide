# Sourcing Policy

## Hierarchy

Prefer, in order: Virginia DCJS; Code of Virginia; Virginia Administrative Code; HRCJTA; James City County and James City County Police; Virginia courts and official judicial materials; authoritative federal sources; respected academic/professional sources; carefully selected books and secondary literature. Commercial preparation sites, blogs, forums, Reddit, social media, unsourced summaries, and old academy materials cannot establish Virginia requirements.

## Claim traceability

A reader must be able to trace every material factual claim requiring support. Cite near the claim in notes or prose and repeat full, locatable entries in the chapter references. Never fabricate a citation, title, date, quotation, URL, rule, or holding. Record uncertainty and unresolved conflicts in `sources/shared/research-log.md`.

## Web records and dates

Record organization or author, exact page/document title, stable URL, publication or revision date when shown, and an access date (`Accessed YYYY-MM-DD`) for changeable web material. Save research notes, not copyrighted copies, unless redistribution is permitted. Check links and effective status during factual review.

## Citation forms

- **Statute:** *Code of Virginia* § section (year/current-through date), official URL, accessed date.
- **Regulation:** title VAC agency-chapter-section, section title, effective/current-through date, Virginia Regulatory Town Hall or official LIS URL, accessed date.
- **Case:** case name, reporter citation, court and year; official opinion URL when available. Explain the holding narrowly and check later treatment.
- **DCJS:** Virginia Department of Criminal Justice Services, exact standard/page/document, revision or effective date/version, page or section, official URL, accessed date.
- **HRCJTA:** Hampton Roads Criminal Justice Training Academy, exact published page/document, publication/revision date if available, URL, accessed date. Do not infer unpublished schedules or practices.
- **James City County:** James City County or James City County Police Department, exact page/document, date if available, URL, accessed date. Do not generalize an employment page into an academy requirement.
- **Secondary work:** author, full title, edition, publisher/journal, year, DOI/ISBN or stable URL, and relevant pages where useful.

## Currency and supersession

> Never silently rely on an outdated standard when a newer standard is currently effective.

Mark a superseded source as historical, name its replacement, and do not use it to state a present rule. Capture the “current through” date used for codes and regulations.

> When a future standard has been adopted but is not yet effective, distinguish the currently effective rule from the future rule and state the future effective date.

If authority conflicts or currency cannot be verified, say so and withhold a definitive claim. English and Spanish normally share the same authority records under `sources/shared/`; an authoritative Spanish resource may be added there when useful.

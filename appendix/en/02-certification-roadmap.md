# Virginia Law-Enforcement Certification Roadmap

This roadmap separates statewide rules, common agency processes, and the limited James City County information verified publicly. It is a map, not a promise that every employer uses the same sequence.

> **Research date: August 20, 2026.** Verify the current statute, regulation, DCJS directions, academy instructions, and employer requirements before acting.

## The pathway at a glance

```text
Explore the profession
        ↓
Apply to an employing law-enforcement agency
        ↓
Hiring, background, and agency requirements
        ↓
Employment or recruit status
        ↓
Certified basic law-enforcement academy
        ↓
Compulsory training, examinations, and qualifications
        ↓
DCJS certification requirements and records
        ↓
Agency field training and probation
        ↓
Authorized working officer
        ↓
Mandatory in-service training and continuing development
```

Arrows show a useful conceptual order. Hiring steps may overlap; an agency may employ a recruit before academy attendance; graduation, certification, field training, probation, and authorization for particular duties remain distinct.

## Who controls what?

| Label | Authority | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| **Statewide requirement** | Code of Virginia, Criminal Justice Services Board regulations, and DCJS | Covered officer qualifications, compulsory training, certification administration, and continuing-training framework. |
| **Common agency process** | Employing agency | Recruitment, selection, background process, appointment, local policy, equipment, assignment, field training, probation, and standards exceeding the state minimum. Details vary. |
| **Academy process** | Certified academy under DCJS requirements | Delivery and documentation of approved instruction and assessment. Academy calendars and local rules vary. |
| **James City County-specific information** | James City County/JCCPD public materials | Recruiting and employment information actually published by the County. A recruiting page is not proof of an unpublished academy or FTO procedure. |

## Stage-by-stage questions

### 1. Explore and apply — common agency process

Read the employing agency's current job posting, minimum qualifications, disqualifiers, application directions, and deadlines. Virginia Code § 15.2-1701 supplies statewide officer qualifications within its coverage, but an employer may impose additional lawful requirements. Ask which requirements apply at application, conditional offer, appointment, academy entry, and continued employment.

### 2. Selection and appointment — statewide rules plus agency process

Background, medical, psychological, interview, testing, and administrative steps may occur in different orders. Do not infer a universal sequence from one vacancy announcement. Obtain written clarification about pay status, academy sponsorship, employment contingencies, and what happens if a requirement is not completed.

### 3. Basic academy — statewide framework; academy delivery

Code of Virginia § 15.2-1706, **6VAC20-20**, and current DCJS directions govern the compulsory-training and certification framework. A certified academy delivers the applicable program. The academy and DCJS—not this book—identify the curriculum version, attendance rules, performance outcomes, examinations, qualifications, and records for a particular cohort.

### 4. Examination, qualification, and certification — distinct milestones

- An academy assessment measures a specified part of academy learning.
- A qualification verifies a defined minimum under prescribed conditions.
- The Virginia certification examination occupies a distinct place in DCJS's process.
- Graduation records academy completion.
- Certification is the state-administered legal/administrative status.

Public material reviewed did **not** justify publishing a universal question count, passing score, attempt limit, or remediation promise. Use current written DCJS and academy instructions.

### 5. Field training and probation — agency processes within the wider framework

Field training places academy learning into supervised real work. Probation is an employment status. They may overlap, but neither term should be treated as a synonym for certification. Ask the agency about assignment, evaluation, duration, scheduling, extension, and completion criteria.

### 6. Working officer and continuing training

Authorization to perform work remains bounded by law, certification status, assignment, supervision, and policy. **6VAC20-30** supplies Virginia's compulsory in-service framework. As reviewed for this edition, it uses a 40-hour requirement within each two-year period for covered officers and separately addresses annual firearms retraining for officers authorized to carry; current eligibility, allocations, exemptions, reporting, and DCJS directions control.

## Current and adopted-future standards

> **Current requirement — as researched August 20, 2026.** The then-effective Code, 6VAC20-20, and DCJS directions control a 2026 cohort.

> **Adopted future requirement — effective January 1, 2027.** DCJS posted revised compulsory minimum training standards and performance outcomes for implementation on this date. They must not be blended into the 2026 requirements. DCJS and the academy must identify transition treatment for a cohort crossing the date.

This research environment could not reopen the official sites during the final appendix audit because its external HTTPS tunnel returned 403 responses. The date distinction was preserved from the authoritative records already reviewed; the precise current file title, revision, and transition direction remain a **prepublication recheck**, not a guessed conclusion.

## James City County / HRCJTA: what was and was not verified

James City County publishes a **Police Jobs** page, and HRCJTA publicly identifies its regional academy role and member-agency context. Those sources support using JCCPD as the prospective employer and HRCJTA as relevant regional context. They do not establish a public daily schedule, physical cutoff, certification score, FTO model, field-training duration, probation length, shift pattern, or continued-employment rule.

> **The exact James City County procedure was not verified from a public authoritative source.** Applicants should obtain the current job announcement and written directions from James City County Human Resources/JCCPD; recruits should follow their academy and chain of command.

# Preface

Considering police work means considering a profession shaped by public trust, law, judgment, communication, physical demands, and responsibility. This book is designed to walk beside an intelligent adult reader—especially a native Spanish speaker with court-system experience—as she learns what Virginia basic law-enforcement education may ask of her.

The nineteen numbered weeks create a readable progression. They do **not** assert that the Hampton Roads Criminal Justice Training Academy teaches these subjects in these particular weeks. Verified requirements and academy- or department-specific statements will be identified and sourced as the manuscript is researched.

This foundation edition contains conspicuous placeholders. It demonstrates the planned journey and publishing system; it is not yet guidance on which a recruit should rely.

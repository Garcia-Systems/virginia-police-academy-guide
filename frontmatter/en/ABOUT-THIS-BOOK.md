# About This Book

## Purpose and reader

The finished book will explain what a prospective Virginia recruit may learn, why it matters, what may be difficult, how court-system experience may provide context, and where to read further. It prepares the reader for understanding—not test-cramming—and never treats prior employment as a substitute for academy or field experience.

## Six kinds of statements

Drafting must distinguish: Virginia law or regulation; Virginia DCJS compulsory minimum standards; HRCJTA-published information; James City County Police-published information; generally accepted training concepts; and this book's illustrative editorial organization.

## Educational disclaimer

This book is supplementary educational material. It is not official instruction, agency policy, legal advice, or a substitute for supervised firearms, emergency-vehicle, defensive-tactics, or medical training. Requirements and policies can change. Readers must follow current law, DCJS requirements, academy instruction, and employing-agency policy.

## Foundation status

All chapter bodies in this first edition are labeled placeholders. No placeholder is a factual claim or a fabricated citation. Research will be added in later, reviewable increments.

Future research will begin with primary authorities, including [Virginia DCJS](https://www.dcjs.virginia.gov/), and will register each source only after direct review.
